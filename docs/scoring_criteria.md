# Scoring Criteria - Final Projects

> English rendering of the organizers' internal criteria (`kryteria_oceny.docx`).
> This is the "5 pillars" half. The full point universe (artifacts + outcome + pitch + audience) is in `02_scoring.md`.

**Maximum: 100 points** (5 pillars × 20)

At the final hackathon your team faces a jury panel acting as the Client and Potential Investor. What is assessed is how effectively you used the knowledge from each day of the marathon to turn a raw problem into a finished, market-ready, working product.

## Criterion Zero - Necessary Condition

The app must fulfil the assigned domain task. The AI tools and implemented algorithms must correctly and logically process the problem and return substantively correct, sensible solutions.

**If the system does not fulfil the core business function (i.e. does not solve the assigned problem), the project will not pass verification with the Client.**

## The 5 Scoring Pillars (20 pts each)

| # | Pillar | Day | Max pts |
|---|--------|-----|---------|
| 1 | **Product Design & MVP** | Day 1 | 20 |
| 2 | **UI/UX & A11Y** | Day 2 | 20 |
| 3 | **Angular, NX, Debugging** | Day 3 | 20 |
| 4 | **Fullstack (NestJS), Architecture, LLM Engineering** | Day 4 | 20 |
| 5 | **Deployment, Scale & Optimization** | Day 5 | 20 |

*Total: 100 points | 5 pillars × 20 pts*

> Note: the official submission scores these as 5 daily artifacts (each with a link). The outcome criteria (Innovation/Usability/Design/Completeness), Pitch, and Audience vote are separate - see `02_scoring.md` and `hackathon-priorities-scoring.md`.
