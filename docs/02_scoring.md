## B. Outcome criteria (50 pts - HackYeah style)

| Criterion | Pts | What it assesses | Owner |
|-----------|-----|------------------|-------|
| **Innovation** | **25** | Originality, unconventional approach, potential for growth and real impact | Oleh (framing) + whole team |
| Usability | 10 | Practicality, intuitiveness, does it really solve the user's problem | Oleh |
| Design | 10 | Purely the visual layer - subjective "is it nice", consistency, polish | Oleh |
| Completeness | 5 | Degree of completion, deployment readiness | Denys |

> **Important:** organizers deliberately gave Completeness only 5 pts, with the comment "finishing a dumb idea does not matter". Innovation (25) is the single highest weight. The signal: **a bold idea at 80% beats a boring one at 100%**.

## C. Other categories (50 pts)

| Criterion | Pts | What it assesses | Owner |
|-----------|-----|------------------|-------|
| **Pitch** | **25** | 5 min before the jury (client/investor), hard timer, no Q&A | Oleh |
| **Audience vote** | **25** | Participants voting after the pitches | Oleh (pitch drives it) |

## D. Rate all teams (required action, NOT part of our 200)

At least 1 team member must rate ALL teams' presentations (form published ~after pitches). This unlocks nothing toward our own quality score in this model - treat it as a mandatory gate, not a point source.

**Owner: Oleh** (not coding in that window). Put it on the task board as a hard task with a deadline.

## Key insight for the PM

The "soft" areas (Innovation, Pitch, Audience vote, Design, Usability) = ~70 pts out of 200, and they sit largely under your ownership. Plus you gate another 100 pts (the daily artifacts) by making sure each one is actually delivered and linked. This is not a side task - it is a large, high-weight competition you largely own.
